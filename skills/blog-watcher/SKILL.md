# Blog Watcher

Monitor RSS/Atom feeds and blogs for new posts and deliver summaries.

## Usage
- "Watch the Hacker News RSS feed"
- "Check for new posts on my favorite blogs"
- "Summarize the latest posts from my feeds"

## How it works
1. Fetches RSS/Atom feeds from configured URLs
2. Detects new entries since the last check
3. Generates summaries of new posts
